Dependencies installed by package managers retain their own licenses. This inventory is not a grant of rights to third-party material.

Liquid's LFM license defines a US$10 million annual-revenue threshold and makes commercial rights conditional on not exceeding it (sections 1 and 5); entities beyond it need a separate grant from Liquid AI. Review the exact boundary and your controlled/controlling entities with Liquid if relevant. Redistribution requires the license, retained attribution/NOTICE where supplied, and prominent modification notices. These are model terms, not an unrestricted MIT model grant.

Source-only publication of this repository does not distribute the downloaded models or certify a deployable binary's compliance. The Lessac ambiguity and the GPL Piper integration must be resolved for the intended product/distribution before claiming unrestricted commercial readiness. This review does not change running services, model selections or repository visibility.
