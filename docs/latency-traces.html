Where the time goes

  • Codex model calls are the biggest cost. A request that uses one tool needs two model calls (decide, then answer), 2–4 s together. First token: median 1.6 s, slowest 10% 5.0 s or more (75 voice turns).
  • GPT-Live adds about 1.3 s. About 0.7 s to notice you stopped and hand off, and 0.6 s from the brain's answer to sound coming out (medians of 105 and 127 events).
  • Image to text has no step of its own. The camera snapshot takes about 10 ms. The cost is the next model call, which reads the JPEG: median 2.8 s after an image result vs 1.85 s after a text result. In the vision trace it took 1.2 s.
  • Web search: 3.5 s median, plus a slow first token on that turn (3.8 s).
  • Background tasks: starting one takes 0.1 s, but the worker thread needs 1.2 s (median, up to 5.9 s) before it starts working, and then makes its own model calls. Whole tasks run 47 s median.
  • The first turn after a voice restart is cold: 3.6 s of turn setup plus a 6.4 s first token (trace 4).
  • Waking from sleep costs about 6.3 s before GPT-Live hears you: 3.2 s to start the realtime session, 1.4 s WebRTC, 1.7 s to replay your held audio.
  • Context compaction stalls the brain 20 s (median, 19 times today, up to 83 s).
